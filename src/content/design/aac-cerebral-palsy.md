People with complex communication needs caused by severe motor disabilities, such as cerebral
palsy, are at special risk of being excluded. This bachelor thesis developed components of an
augmentative and alternative communication (AAC) system to raise the success rate of their
conversations.

## What I made

- A personal scanning device that lets a user with severe motor disability scan, select and
  print symbols on an electronic display with a single access switch.
- A spectacle-mounted laser pointer for direct selection on a communication board.
- A head-controlled mouse for better computer access.

All research and user testing took place with the educators, AAC developers, students and day
care attendees of the Indian Institute of Cerebral Palsy. Prototypes were tested with experienced
AAC users. The brief asked for components that are cheap and can be produced locally.

**Tools:** sketching as research, CAD, laser cutting, Arduino, electronics.
