Urban development in Indian cities rarely asks children what they want, even though they are the
city's future residents. This project tried to break the barrier between children and policy makers
and give children a stronger sense of belonging in their own city.

The work produced a facilitator's guide for participatory workshops with children, a set of activity
cards that build decision-making skills, and a gigamap of the research. The journey was recorded on a
[blog](https://scribbleonthesidewalk.wordpress.com).

Made with Binayak Mandal, Mridu Jhangiani and Subhrajit Ghosal.
