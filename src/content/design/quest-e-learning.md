India's growth depends on the employability of its young people, yet for many from lower income
groups career planning hardly exists. With Quest Alliance we made "mobisodes", short mobile
episodes that give 15 to 21 year olds the mindset, skills and knowledge to shape their own careers.

## Process

1. Sorting content into modules and breaking it into mobisodes with a narrative each.
2. Auditing content and language to write the final scripts.
3. Designing each mobisode: illustrations, animation, typography and sound.
4. Testing with learners for usability and transfer.

Each of the 7 modules has its own theme and characters, which let us tell different stories per
lesson around the same cast. The courses are live on [questapp.in](https://questapp.in/).

## World of Work micro-website

An interactive collection of career profiles in the same design language. Users record their
goals and see how well they suit each sector. Live at [wow.wildfire.world](https://wow.wildfire.world/).
