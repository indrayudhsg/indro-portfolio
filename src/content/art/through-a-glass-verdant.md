The native of the anthropocene imagines a verdant forest, vines cascading from boughs weighed down
by fruit, a myriad lifeforms crawling, slithering, scrambling, and flapping. "This is nature," they
say with a sigh. From parks, gardens, ponds and lakes to houseplants, aquariums and terrariums, the
nostalgia for the natural haunts their urban environment.

This project explores the contradiction of a well-intentioned practice of nurturing nature
confronted with the artifice of the creation itself. Using the non-living parts that constitute the
aquarium (glass, pipes, pumps, lights) as raw material, I try to capture my impressions and memories
of the living non-human. The glass that usually separates the human from the aquatic becomes the
very medium which documents the foreign flora and fauna that I have touched before.
